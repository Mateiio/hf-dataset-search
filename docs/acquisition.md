# Acquisition: DataONE coverage of EDI

Measured 2026-09-13 by `python -m edisearch.acquire.probe`. Every number below is regenerated by that command; the listings it works from are in `data/probe/` (not committed, a few MB). The object-store sweep takes about 8 minutes; the per-package portal check takes about two hours and is resumable.

## Why DataONE

PASTA+, EDI's own API, refuses every anonymous call including `readMetadata` (`User 'EDI-... (Public Access)' is not authorized`, re-confirmed by curl on 2026-09-13). EDI is a DataONE member node and the coordinating node serves the same EML anonymously. See `edisearch/acquire/dataone.py` for the four things about DataONE the harvester has to handle.

## Headline

EDI's portal reports two tiers on its home page: **contributed** data packages (10,645 unique, 31,579 across all revisions) and **total including EcoTrends and Landsat** (47,011 unique, 83,348 all revisions). The plan's figure of 42,651 was the total tier. `ecotrends`, `lter-landsat` and `lter-landsat-ledaps` are bulk-loaded derived products, roughly 36,000 packages; the search space that matters for retrieval is the contributed tier.

## What the sweep saw

- 385,615 EML objects in the object store across every member node, all revisions: `eml-2.2.0` 79,609, `eml-2.1.1` 112,962, `eml-2.1.0` 70,120, `eml-2.0.1` 117,719, `eml-2.0.0` 5,205. 385,528 of them attributable to a member node
- 158,332 carry a PASTA package id: 76,186 in URL form, 33,260 bare, 48,886 as `doi:10.6073/AA/...`. They collapse to 59,359 distinct series; revisions per series median 1, max 138
- PASTA ids by node: LTER 133,129, KNB 6,194, TFRI 5,600, EDI 3,557, SANPARKS 2,431, PPBIO 1,673, SFWMD 1,653, CERP_SFWMD 1,153, GOA 1,126, ARCTIC 1,115, LTER_EUROPE 343, ESA 224, IOE 85, OTS_NDC 42, PISCO 4, CLOEBIRD 3
- **The Solr index is not the object store.** An earlier pass of this probe used `query/solr` and found 97,223 PASTA ids in 260,389 indexed EML documents; `listObjects` finds the counts above. The index had `edi.134` at revision 10 while revisions 11 and 12 existed as objects and the portal listed 12 as current. The index also carries broken `obsoletedBy` chains across identifier spellings (7,063 older revisions with no successor recorded), so neither `-obsoletedBy:*` nor the index itself is a way to find current revisions. The rule this probe and the harvester use is: highest revision per series in the object store
- **Stale is permanent, not lag.** The 10 stale `knb-lter-hfr` packages have no newer object in DataONE at all (checked by asking for system metadata at the next five revisions), and DataONE last touched some of them in 2021. Roughly 3% of packages will carry an older revision's metadata than the portal shows, and only a PASTA+ account would close that gap
